ABOUT YASTER

A business bridge from South India to the world.

Yaster International, based in Puducherry, India, connects global business opportunities with reliable resources from India. Our two core divisions — Manpower Solutions and Import & Export — support overseas employers, recruitment partners, importers, wholesalers and institutional buyers.

We focus on clear communication, dependable coordination and long-term B2B relationships, from understanding requirements and identifying suitable candidates or products through to documentation and ongoing business support.
